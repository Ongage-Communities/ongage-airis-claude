# Ongage Airis for Claude

Run your email and SMS marketing from Claude. Ongage Airis connects Claude to your Ongage account: ask how your last email did, plan the week, and build segments, emails and automations in your brand, then send and schedule them within your plan's monthly emails. Claude asks before anything is sent, changed or deleted.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, open its Connectors tab and connect Ongage Airis.
2. Enter your email (or use Google) and the 6-digit code Airis emails you, then click Allow. New emails get a free account.
3. Ask: "How did my last email do?", "What should I send this week?", "Build a welcome email in my brand."

New to Airis? Claude asks for your website, and Airis builds your brand book and your first draft emails in a few minutes.

Skills: `airis` (setup and working rules) and five work modes: `analyst`, `creative`, `strategist`, `advisor` and `campaign`.

## Data

The skills are text only and run nothing on your computer. The connector sends your requests to Ongage Airis at api.os.ongage.com, which reads and changes your own Ongage account with the access you approved at sign-in. Privacy policy: https://airis.ongage.com/privacy. Support: support@ongage.com.
